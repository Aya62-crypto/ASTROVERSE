02 / COSMIC BEGINNINGS

How did it all begin?

Scientists use observations, physics and models to investigate how the universe and its contents evolved.

13.8
BILLION YEARS
THE EXPANDING UNIVERSE
01

The Big Bang model

The universe has expanded from an extremely hot, dense early state. Space itself has expanded; this was not an explosion from one spot into empty space.

Strongly supported by cosmic expansion, the cosmic microwave background and the abundance of light elements.
02

Steady State theory

An older model proposing that the universe looks broadly the same on large scales at all times, with matter continuously created as it expands.

It is not supported by the evidence as well as the Big Bang model.
03

Inflation hypothesis

A proposed period of extremely rapid expansion very early in the universe's history. It helps explain several features of the observable universe.

Researchers continue testing different versions of inflation.
04

Multiverse ideas

Some theories suggest our universe could be one of many. This remains speculative; there is no confirmed observational proof of other universes.

An active idea in theoretical physics, not an established discovery.
01

Stars are born

Gravity pulls gas and dust in a nebula together. A hot, dense core forms; when fusion begins, a star is born.

02

Planets take shape

Dust grains in a young star's disc collide and grow into larger bodies, eventually building planets.

03

Stars reach their end

Depending on their mass, stars can become white dwarfs, neutron stars or black holes. Massive stars may explode as supernovae.

06 / QUICK DISCOVERIES

Space is stranger than fiction.

Surprising facts from the worlds and objects around us.

🪐

A day longer than a year

Venus rotates so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one orbit around the Sun.

🌙

Moonquakes happen

Apollo seismometers recorded seismic activity on the Moon. Some moonquakes can last much longer than typical earthquakes.

⭐

Stellar leftovers

A teaspoon of neutron-star material would have an enormous mass on Earth—though such material cannot simply be collected in a spoon.

🛰️

Space is not silent

Sound needs a medium such as air, so it cannot travel through the vacuum of space. Spacecraft can convert data into audio for us to hear.
